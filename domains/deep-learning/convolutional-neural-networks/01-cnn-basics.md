# 1. CNN Basics

> Spatial feature learners — weight sharing and local receptive fields.

## Definition

**Convolutional neural networks (CNNs)** use convolution filters that slide over spatial (or sequential) inputs, sharing weights and capturing local patterns.

## Why CNNs

| Property | Benefit |
|----------|---------|
| Local connectivity | Inductive bias for images |
| Weight sharing | Fewer parameters |
| Hierarchy | Edges → textures → objects |
