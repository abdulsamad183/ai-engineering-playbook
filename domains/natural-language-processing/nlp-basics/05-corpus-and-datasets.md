# 5. Corpus & Datasets

> Collections of text — domains, splits, licenses, and leakage risks.

## Definition

A **corpus** is a collection of texts used for training or evaluation. Dataset quality and splits dominate NLP outcomes.

## Checklist

- License & PII  
- Domain match to production  
- Train/val/test without leakage  
- Label guidelines for supervised tasks
