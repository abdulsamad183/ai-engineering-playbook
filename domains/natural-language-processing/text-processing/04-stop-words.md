# 4. Stop Words

> High-frequency function words — drop carefully for sparse models.

## Definition

**Stop words** are common tokens (`the`, `a`, `is`) often removed for BoW/TF-IDF. Neural models usually keep them.

## Caution

- Sentiment and negation (`not`) can live in "stop" lists — don't blindly drop
