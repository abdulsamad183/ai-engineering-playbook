# 1. Why Transformers

> What recurrence struggled with — and why attention scaled better.

## Definition

**Transformers** replaced sequential RNN bottlenecks with parallelizable self-attention, unlocking large-scale pretraining.

## Wins

| vs RNNs | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Parallel over tokens | Faster training |
| Direct long-range links | Better dependency modeling |
| Scalable stacks | Foundation models |
