# 4. Pre-Norm vs Post-Norm

> Where to put LayerNorm — modern stacks prefer pre-norm.

## Definition

**Pre-norm** normalizes before attention/FFN sublayers; **post-norm** after. Pre-norm is usually easier to train at scale.
