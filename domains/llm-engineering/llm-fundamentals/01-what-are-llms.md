# 1. What are LLMs

> Large neural language models trained to predict tokens at massive scale.

## Definition

A **large language model (LLM)** is a neural network (usually a decoder-only transformer) trained on vast text to predict the next token. Emergent abilities appear as scale and data grow.

## Engineering view

- Input: tokens (+ optional images/tools)  
- Output: next-token distribution → sampled text  
- Value: general-purpose reasoning/writing interface via prompts
